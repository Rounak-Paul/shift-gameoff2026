How to play

↔

Slide a row. It wraps.

↻

Turn the cartridge. Columns become rows.

● ● ●

Match 3 or more in a row or column. Cascades multiply points.

Q / E or ← / → to turn · W / S or ↑ / ↓ to select · A / D to slide · H for a hint · Esc to cancel a drag or queued turn

In levels, each row slide uses one move, regardless of distance. Turns and hints are free. A/D follows the full hinted slide in that direction. Complete every goal before moves run out; cascades finish before the result.
